# Independent-cell streaming information calibration

Study: `repair-qwen-independent-trace-development-v1`. This prospective protocol
is separate from every closed bank. It neither changes their gates nor fills
missing cells. Code: `vao.repair.independent_information_study`,
`independent_streaming`, `vao.analysis.repair_missingness`. The original human
goal remains GOAL-AMENDMENT-20261026.md; no selector is trained in this study.

## Target and exposure

Measure the finite selected-cell contrast in full repair probability for focused
public feedback plus a bounded public trace versus the same feedback alone.
Retain all twelve original 27B-generated source checkpoints and public-only
qualification: eight existing public failures are eligible, seven youtube-dl and
one httpie; the other four public passes remain in provenance, including known
evaluator failures/specification ambiguity. All earlier outcomes are exposed.
These are development checkpoints, not fresh or held-out bugs. No favorable
pooling, historical controls or causal model-size/transport comparison.

Four draws per arm per eligible bug give 32 paired decisions and 64 NEW requests.
Shuffle pairs with seed 43136 and alternate arm ordering (16 pairs each order).
Both pair members have the same generation seed, model/provider, precision,
code, public feedback, output contract and limits; only `runtime_diagnostic`
differs. Hidden evaluator data never enter qualification, diagnostics, messages
or action decisions. All provider bodies/attempt IDs must be disjoint from all
556 unique planned bodies in fourteen frozen archives, including unsent bodies.

## Fixed model and serving contract

Use qwen/qwen3-coder-next, Parasail/BF16, JSON-current, temperature 0.7, maximum
4096 output tokens, 32768-token input reservation, pricing ceilings 0.12/0.80 USD
per million input/output tokens, no fallback. Pin streaming with an explicit
8 MiB envelope, 180-second socket idle timeout and 300-second wall check at read
boundaries (not a hard deadline). Require complete final usage and DONE for a
receipt. Space new requests by at least 60 seconds. Public feedback and bounded
traces must reproduce before any dispatch. Frozen clean source, exact requests,
review, full tests and green CI precede a one-use launch claim.

## Prospectively bounded independent-cell disposition

A successful receipt, including a billed invalid or truncated artifact, resets
only the consecutive network-error counter. Scientific outcomes never affect
request order or whether to proceed. On an allowlisted no-receipt network error,
retain the request as missing and its full reservation; do not retry, substitute
or resume it. Wait at least 120 seconds, record the measured cooldown and hashes
of the error and stream metric, then permit only the next independently scheduled,
never-admitted request. At most seven errors may unlock a subsequent request:
the **eighth total** or **third consecutive** network error stops the entire study.

The allowlist is TimeoutError, URLError, RemoteDisconnected, IncompleteRead and
ConnectionResetError during awaiting_response_headers or reading_stream. No HTTP
status, raw/settled receipt, reported usage/cost, final DONE or parser error may
be present. HTTP/authentication, pricing, identity, framing/parser, accounting,
local verifier and all other errors remain terminal. A reported amount received
before a failed terminal marker must still be counted, even though that error
cannot unlock another cell. No key rotation, fallback, retry, resumption or
successor. A crash during cooldown leaves the durable reserve and no further
admission. Never act from partial generated code.

If the schedule is exhausted with unknown outcomes, record a terminal incomplete
bank, not a successful complete seal. Keep every selected cell, including all
never-admitted cells after an operational stop. While the process is active,
inspect only accounting, request identity, errors and progress; no treatment
statistics, bounds, gate, outcome-dependent stopping or tuning.

## Separate bounded allocation

Declare a separate **0.47 USD** allocation under standing user instructions to
continue and the retained **0.60 USD** cumulative ceiling. This is not a newly
user-typed amount or reuse of a closed allocation. Carry **0.12585127 USD** once,
including all **thirteen historical unresolved reservations**. Full allocation
plus carry is **0.59585127 USD**; 64 reservations of 0.00720896 total 0.46137344,
maximum planned cumulative commitment **0.58722471 USD**. Provider preflight must
verify available capacity before admission; this protocol creates no new funds.

Every request is durably reserved before dispatch. For unknowns count the larger
of reserve and any trustworthy observed charge. All failed/truncated replies and
paid outcomes lacking evaluation remain accounted. Counterfactual source-prefix
cost, public verification, diagnostic execution and response verification time
are reported separately from actual API spending. Equal per-call limits do not
establish equal realized spending or equal-total-resource policy performance.

## Prespecified analysis under missingness

Only primary contrast: `focused_trace_minus_focused` on all 64 selected cells,
with bugs equally weighted. Report all twelve sources, complete repairs, public
false acceptances, regressions, unusable outputs, missingness and every cost.
A complete uncapped bank retains the mean and descriptive 10,000-resample paired
bug-cluster bootstrap (seed 43123). Any incomplete bank keeps mean/bootstrap
null; no complete-case or imputed mean replaces them.

For planned cells per arm n, observed successes sT/sC and missing cells mT/mC,
report finite bounds [(sT − sC − mC)/n, (sT + mT − sC)/n]. These are not confidence
intervals and require no random-missingness assumption. They describe only the
selected bank, not the population of bugs.

The efficacy thresholds remain: at least three bugs with two trace successes
each, at least four net additional trace successes, and strictly positive net
trace advantage in both draw halves {0,1} and {2,3}. This new development screen
passes ONLY if all thresholds hold for EVERY binary completion of missing cells:
assign all missing trace outcomes to failure and all missing focused outcomes to
success for the least-favorable screen. These monotone thresholds are jointly
minimized by that assignment. A passed lower screen permits separate independent
calibration, never automatic selector training.

The opposite assignment jointly maximizes all thresholds. If even this upper
screen fails, label `no_completion_meets_development_screen`; that rejects only
the finite screen and is not proof of zero population benefit. Otherwise label
`inconclusive_selected_cell_bounds`. A cap overrun is inconclusive regardless of
the screen. Report both extremes and the original missing values, never imputed
cells. No claim that a positive screen generalizes or predicts action value.
Old studies retain their original mandatory-completeness gates unchanged.

## Terminal verification and limits

Audit the ordered ledger, each retention error/metric/cooldown, actual provider
bodies, receipt/usage identities and all pending costs. Export inert frozen
sources and independently reexecute saved public/evaluator tests and all public
diagnostics; reproduce analysis and Markdown exactly and inspect the figures.
Raw SSE bytes are not saved: saved wire hashes do not imply byte-level replay.
Publish nulls, bounds, false positives, regressions and all missing cells.

The protocol addresses how evidence is collected under recurrent infrastructure
loss; it does not establish that transport reliability is repaired. Same exposed
sources and observed earlier results limit interpretation. Even a favorable
screen requires independent bugs, repeated benefit, all-in resource accounting,
fixed strategies and a prospective selector tested on untouched grouped bugs.
